Traditional Chinese (繁體中文) and Simplified Chinese (简体中文) translation for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820790624]Weazel Mode[/url] by WhereWolf.

為 WhereWolf 的 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820790624]Weazel Mode[/url] 提供繁體中文與簡體中文翻譯。

[h2]What is translated[/h2][list]
[*]Race, faction, pawn kinds, traders and backstories
[*]Items, apparel, weapons, buildings and research
[*]Health conditions, drugs, thoughts, genes and the scenario
[/list]
Translated from the mod's English text, with the Korean original checked for names. Terms follow the vanilla Chinese translations. The Simplified Chinese version is converted from the Traditional Chinese one, with game terms replaced by the vanilla Simplified Chinese names.

[h2]翻譯範圍[/h2][list]
[*]種族、派系、角色類型、商人與背景故事
[*]物品、衣物、武器、建築與研究
[*]健康狀態、藥物、心情、基因與劇本
[/list]
以模組的英文文本為來源翻譯，專有名詞對照韓文原文。用詞依照原版遊戲的繁體中文翻譯。

[h2]简体中文[/h2]简体中文版由繁体中文版转换而来，游戏用语已替换为原版简体中文的译名。如有用词不自然之处，敬请见谅。

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Load after Weazel Mode.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[*]A few messages compiled into the original mod's DLL (equipment restrictions, Essence extraction warning) stay in Korean until the author rebuilds it.
[*]Text added in later Weazel Mode updates shows in English or Korean until the translation is updated.
[/list]
I made this because I wanted Weazel Mode in my game and no Chinese translation existed yet. If anyone would like to take over the translation, you are welcome to.
